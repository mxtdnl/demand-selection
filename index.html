Two decks. Your free choice.

You'll repeatedly pick one of two patches. Each pick shows a single digit to judge. Nothing more. First, learn the two judgments — then it's your choice, over and over.

◆ Blue digit — PARITY

Is the number odd or even?

F = Odd  ·  J = Even
● Orange digit — MAGNITUDE

Is the number less than 5 or greater than 5?

F = < 5  ·  J = > 5

The colour of the digit tells you which judgment to make. Use the keyboard (F / J) or the on-screen buttons. (The number 5 never appears.)

The hidden truth

The decks looked identical — but one quietly demanded more. It switched the judgment rule far more often, and rule-switching is the effort you couldn't see.

Corroboration — the cost of switching

If the harder deck really cost more, your responses there should be slower and a little more error-prone.
